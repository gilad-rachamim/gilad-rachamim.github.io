        Brit milah honors: what each honor is, and who receives it

        The honors at a brit milah are the way to include family and friends in the mitzvah. Every brit has a few fixed honors, and every community has its own additions. The tool above arranges them in the order in which they happen in the ceremony, so that every honoree knows when his turn comes, and the mohel can call him by name without searching.

        Kvater

        The kvater brings the baby from the mother to the guests and passes him from hand to hand until he reaches the father. Several people can be honored with this, and in the Ashkenazi custom a couple is honored: a kvater and a kvaterin, usually a couple who are still waiting for children, as a segulah. A good honor for grandparents, siblings and close friends.

        Elijah's chair

        The baby is placed for a moment on the chair of Elijah the prophet, the angel of the covenant, and then taken from it and handed to the father. These are two separate honors, and each can go to a different person.

        Sandak

        The sandak holds the baby on his knees during the circumcision, and this is the most important honor at the brit. In many families it goes to the grandfather, and there are customs about who is sandak for the first son and who for the second. It is worth settling this in the family in advance, so that no one is hurt.

        Standing for the blessings (Ashkenazi custom)

        In the Ashkenazi and Chabad custom, someone is honored to hold the baby standing during the blessing over the cup and the naming; he is also called the "standing sandak".

        Kiddush and the naming

        After the circumcision a blessing is said over a cup of wine, followed by the prayer in which the baby is named in Israel. One person can be honored with both, or they can be split: one says the blessing over the cup and another recites the naming. In the Mizrahi communities and among Yemenite Jews it is also customary to say the blessing over the spices (besamim), usually myrtle, and this too is an honor.

        What to bring to a brit milah

        Most of the equipment is simple, but on a day like this it is easy to forget something. The list in the tool has everything you need: a brit cushion, dry red wine and a cup, two tallitot for the father and the sandak, spices or myrtle, a siddur, a diaper bag with cloth diapers and a change of clothes, and comfortable two-piece clothing for the baby, so that only the bottom can be opened.         What to prepare in advance for a brit milah

        A few days before: agree on a place and time with the mohel, work out the date of the brit (Hebrew), choose a sandak and a kvater, send an invitation to the brit (Hebrew) and order food for the festive meal. The evening before: pack the bag from the list, charge the phone and send each honoree the honors card, so that everyone arrives on time.

        Questions and answers

        What are the honors at a brit milah?

        The usual honors: kvater, placing the baby on Elijah's chair, taking him from the chair and handing him to the father, sandak, the blessing over the cup (Kiddush) and the naming. In the Ashkenazi custom there is also standing for the blessings (the "standing sandak"), and in the Mizrahi communities and among Yemenite Jews the blessing over the spices.

        What should I bring to a brit milah?

        A brit cushion, red wine and a cup for Kiddush, two tallitot for the father and the sandak, spices or myrtle by custom, a diaper bag with cloth diapers and a change of clothes, and comfortable two-piece clothing for the baby. The tool has a full list with check marks that is saved on your phone.

        What should I prepare for a brit milah?

        Agree on a place and time with the mohel, choose a sandak and a kvater and hand out the other honors, send an invitation, order food for the festive meal, and prepare the equipment from the list already the evening before.

        What care does the baby need after a brit milah?

        Follow the mohel's instructions: usually the dressing is removed after a few hours or the next day, ointment is applied at every diaper change, and the diaper is changed often. Any bleeding, fever or unusual restlessness – call the mohel at once.

        How much money should I bring to a brit milah?

        Guests who bring a gift usually give a smaller sum than at a wedding, and relatives give more. There is no fixed sum, and a gift for the baby is also customary.

        How does a mohel brand the honors card?

        Choose "I am a mohel", write a name and phone, and they appear at the bottom of the card. "Send to the parents" creates a link, and the parents open the card with your name and fill in the names of the honorees.

        Are the details saved?

        Yes, only on your phone or computer. Nothing is sent to a server, and the sharing link carries the card inside it.
