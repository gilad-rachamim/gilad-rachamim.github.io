The Hanukkah blessings

Say the blessings before lighting, while the shamash (the helper candle) is already lit and the candles are ready. After each blessing, those listening answer “Amen”.

Blessing 1 – every night: to kindle the Hanukkah light

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה', אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֹתָיו, וְצִוָּנוּ לְהַדְלִיק נֵר חֲנֻכָּה:

Say: Baruch ata A-donai, eloheinu melech ha'olam, asher kideshanu bemitzvotav, vetzivanu lehadlik ner chanuka.

Meaning: Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has sanctified us with His commandments, and commanded us to kindle the Hanukkah light.

Blessing 2 – every night: who performed miracles

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה', אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁעָשָׂה נִסִּים לַאֲבוֹתֵֽינוּ בַּיָּמִים הָהֵם בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה:

Say: Baruch ata A-donai, eloheinu melech ha'olam, she'asa nisim la'avoteinu bayamim hahem bazeman haze.

Meaning: Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who performed miracles for our ancestors in those days, at this time.

Blessing 3 – first night only: Shehecheyanu

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה', אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁהֶחֱיָֽינוּ וְקִיְּמָנוּ וְהִגִּיעָנוּ לַזְּמַן הַזֶּה:

Say: Baruch ata A-donai, eloheinu melech ha'olam, shehecheyanu vekiyemanu vehigi'anu lazeman haze.

Meaning: Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has granted us life, sustained us, and enabled us to reach this season.

Many Moroccan families say לְהַדְלִיק נֵר שֶׁל חֲנֻכָּה (lehadlik ner shel chanuka) in the first blessing. Whoever has a custom from their family keeps it.

Haneirot Halalu: after lighting

Haneirot Halalu – said after the first candle is lit

הַנֵּרוֹת הַלָּלוּ אֲנַחְנוּ מַדְלִיקִין עַל הַנִּסִּים, וְעַל הַתְּשׁוּעוֹת, וְעַל הַנִּפְלָאוֹת, שֶׁעָשִׂיתָ לַאֲבוֹתֵֽינוּ עַל יְדֵי כֹּהֲנֶיךָ הַקְּדוֹשִׁים. וְכָל־שְׁמוֹנַת יְמֵי חֲנֻכָּה הַנֵּרוֹת הַלָּלוּ קֹדֶשׁ, וְאֵין לָנוּ רְשׁוּת לְהִשְׁתַּמֵּשׁ בָּהֶם, אֶלָּא לִרְאוֹתָם בִּלְבָד כְּדֵי לְהוֹדוֹת לִשְׁמֶךָ עַל נִסֶּיךָ נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָ וִישׁוּעוֹתֶיךָ:

Say: Hanerot halalu anachnu madlikin al hanisim, ve'al hateshu'ot, ve'al hanifla'ot, she'asita la'avoteinu al yedei kohanecha hakedoshim. Vechol shemonat yemei chanuka hanerot halalu kodesh, ve'ein lanu reshut lehishtamesh bahem, ela lir'otam bilvad kedei lehodot lishmecha al nisecha nifle'otecha vishu'otecha.

Meaning: We kindle these lights for the miracles, for the redemptions, and for the wonders which You performed for our forefathers, through Your holy priests. During all eight days of Hanukkah these lights are sacred, and we are not permitted to make ordinary use of them, but only to look at them, in order to give thanks to Your Name for Your miracles, Your wonders and Your salvations.

Maoz Tzur: the Hanukkah song

Maoz Tzur – first stanza

מָעוֹז צוּר יְשׁוּעָתִי לְךָ נָאֶה לְשַׁבֵּחַ. תִּכּוֹן בֵּית תְּפִלָּתִי וְשָׁם תּוֹדָה נְזַבֵּחַ. לְעֵת תָּכִין מַטְבֵּחַ מִצָּר הַמְנַבֵּחַ. אָז אֶגְמוֹר בְּשִׁיר מִזְמוֹר חֲנֻכַּת הַמִּזְבֵּחַ:

Say: Ma'oz tzur yeshu'ati lecha na'e leshabeach. Tikon beit tefilati vesham toda nezabeach. Le'et tachin matbeach mitzar hamnabeach. Az egmor beshir mizmor chanukat hamizbeach.

Meaning: Rock of my salvation, to praise You is fitting; Restore my house of prayer, and there a thank offering we will sacrifice. When You have prepared a slaughter of the barking foe, then I will complete with song and psalm the dedication of the altar.

רָעוֹת שָׂבְעָה נַפְשִׁי בְּיָגוֹן כֹּחִי כִלָה חַיַּי מֵרְרוּ בְקֹשִׁי בְּשִׁעְבּוּד מַלְכוּת עֶגְלָה וּבְיָדוֹ הַגְּדוֹלָה הוֹצִיא אֶת הַסְּגֻלָּה חֵיל פַּרְעֹה וְכָל זַרְעוֹ יָרְדוּ כְּאֶבֶן בִּמְצוּלָה:

Say: Ra'ot sav'a nafshi beyagon kochi chila chayai mereru vekoshi beshibud malchut egla uvyado hagedola hotzi et hasegula cheil par'o vechol zar'o yardu ke'even bimtzula.

Meaning: My soul is sated with troubles, my strength is spent with sorrow. They embittered my life with hardship, with the enslavement of the kingdom of Eglah. But with His mighty hand, He brought forth the treasured ones. Pharaoh's host and all his offspring descended like a stone into the depths.

דְּבִיר קָדְשׁוֹ הֱבִיאַנִי וְגַם שָׁם לֹא שָׁקַטְתִּי וּבָא נוֹגֵשׂ וְהִגְלַנִי כִּי זָרִים עָבַדְתִּי וְיֵין רַעַל מָסַכְתִּי כִּמְעַט שֶׁעָבַרְתִּי קֵץ בָּבֶל זְרֻבָּבֶל לְקֵץ שִׁבְעִים נוֹשַׁעְתִּי:

Say: Devir kodsho hevi'ani vegam sham lo shakatti uva noges vehiglani ki zarim avadti veyein ra'al masachti kim'at she'avarti ketz bavel zerubavel leketz shiv'im noshati.

Meaning: He brought me to the chamber of His sanctuary, but even there I found no rest. An oppressor came and exiled me because I served foreign gods and mixed poisoned wine. I nearly passed the end; with Zerubbabel, at the end of seventy years, I was saved.

כְּרוֹת קוֹמַת בְּרוֹשׁ בִּקֵּשׁ אֲגָגִי בֶּן הַמְּדָתָא וְנִהְיָתָה לוֹ לְפַח וּלְמוֹקֵשׁ וְגַאֲוָתוֹ נִשְׁבָּתָה רֹאשׁ יְמִינִי נִשֵּׂאתָ וְאוֹיֵב שְׁמוֹ מָחִיתָ רֹב בָּנָיו וְקִנְיָנָיו עַל הָעֵץ תָּלִיתָ:

Say: Kerot komat berosh bikesh agagi ben hamedata venihyata lo lefach ulmokesh vega'avato nishbata rosh yemini niseta ve'oyev shemo machita rov banav vekinyanav al ha'etz talita.

Meaning: Agagite, son of Hammedatha, sought to cut down the stature of cypress, but it became a snare and a trap for him; his pride was brought to an end. My right hand was exalted; you blotted out the name of the enemy; you hanged the multitude of his sons and possessions on the tree.

יְוָנִים נִקְבְּצוּ עָלַי אֲזַי בִּימֵי חַשְׁמַנִּים וּפָרְצוּ חוֹמוֹת מִגְדָּלַי וְטִמְּאוּ כָּל־הַשְּׁמָנִים וּמִנּוֹתַר קַנְקַנִּים נַעֲשָׂה נֵס לַשּׁוֹשַׁנִּים בְּנֵי בִינָה יְמֵי שְׁמוֹנָה קָבְעוּ שִׁיר וּרְנָנִים:

Say: Yevanim nikbetzu alai azai bimei chashmanim ufartzu chomot migdalai vetime'u kol hashemanim uminotar kankanim na'asa nes lashoshanim benei vina yemei shemona kav'u shir urnanim.

Meaning: Greeks gathered against me then, in the days of the Hasmoneans, and they breached the walls of my towers and defiled all the oils. But from the remaining flasks, a miracle was made for the heroes. Sons of understanding established eight days for song and rejoicing.

חֲשׂוֹף זְרוֹעַ קָדְשֶׁךָ וְקָרֵב קֵץ הַיְשׁוּעָה נְקֹם נִקְמַת עֲבָדֶיךָ מֵאֻמָּה הָרְשָׁעָה כִּי אָרְכָה לָנוּ הַשָּׁעָה וְאֵין קֵץ לִימֵי הָרָעָה דְּחֵה אַדְמוֹן בְּצֵל צַלְמוֹן הָקֵם לָנוּ רוֹעֶה שִׁבְעָה:

Say: Chasof zeroa kodshecha vekarev ketz hayeshu'a nekom nikmat avadecha me'uma harsha'a ki orcha lanu hasha'a ve'ein ketz limei hara'a deche admon betzel tzalmon hakem lanu ro'e shiv'a.

Meaning: Reveal your holy arm, and hasten the time of salvation; avenge the vengeance of your servants on the wicked nation, for the hour is long for us and there is no end to the days of evil. Thrust down the red one in the shadow of Zalmon; raise up for us the seven shepherds.

Psalm 30: A Song for the Dedication of the House

מִזְמ֡וֹר (תהילים ל׳:א׳-ב׳) שִׁיר־חֲנֻכַּ֖ת הַבַּ֣יִת לְדָוִֽד׃ אֲרוֹמִמְךָ֣ ה' כִּ֣י דִלִּיתָ֑נִי וְלֹֽא־שִׂמַּ֖חְתָּ אֹיְבַ֣י לִֽי׃ ה' אֱלֹהָ֑י שִׁוַּ֥עְתִּי אֵ֝לֶ֗יךָ וַתִּרְפָּאֵֽנִי׃ ה' הֶעֱלִ֣יתָ מִן־שְׁא֣וֹל נַפְשִׁ֑י חִ֝יִּיתַ֗נִי מיורדי [מִיׇּֽרְדִי]־בֽוֹר׃ זַמְּר֣וּ לַה' חֲסִידָ֑יו וְ֝הוֹד֗וּ לְזֵ֣כֶר קׇדְשֽׁוֹ׃ כִּ֤י רֶ֨גַע ׀ בְּאַפּוֹ֮ חַיִּ֢ים בִּרְצ֫וֹנ֥וֹ בָּ֭עֶרֶב יָלִ֥ין בֶּ֗כִי וְלַבֹּ֥קֶר רִנָּֽה׃ וַ֭אֲנִי אָמַ֣רְתִּי בְשַׁלְוִ֑י בַּל־אֶמּ֥וֹט לְעוֹלָֽם׃ ה' בִּרְצוֹנְךָ֮ הֶעֱמַ֢דְתָּה לְֽהַרְרִ֫י עֹ֥ז הִסְתַּ֥רְתָּ פָנֶ֗יךָ הָיִ֥יתִי נִבְהָֽל׃ אֵלֶ֣יךָ ה' אֶקְרָ֑א וְאֶל־ה' אֶתְחַנָּֽן׃ מַה־בֶּ֥צַע בְּדָמִי֮ בְּרִדְתִּ֢י אֶ֫ל שָׁ֥חַת הֲיוֹדְךָ֥ עָפָ֑ר הֲיַגִּ֥יד אֲמִתֶּֽךָ׃ שְׁמַע־ה' וְחׇנֵּ֑נִי ה' הֱֽיֵה־עֹזֵ֥ר לִֽי׃ הָפַ֣כְתָּ מִסְפְּדִי֮ לְמָח֢וֹל לִ֥י פִּתַּ֥חְתָּ שַׂקִּ֑י וַֽתְּאַזְּרֵ֥נִי שִׂמְחָֽה׃ לְמַ֤עַן ׀ יְזַמֶּרְךָ֣ כָ֭בוֹד וְלֹ֣א יִדֹּ֑ם ה' אֱ֝לֹהַ֗י לְעוֹלָ֥ם אוֹדֶֽךָּ׃

Say: Mizmor shir chanukat habayit ledavid. Aromimcha A-donai ki dilitani velo simachta oyevai li. A-donai elohai shivati elecha vatirpa'eni. A-donai he'elita min she'ol nafshi chiyitani מיורדי [miyordi] vor. Zameru lA-donai chasidav vehodu lezecher kodsho. Ki rega be'apo chayim birtzono ba'erev yalin bechi velaboker rina. Va'ani amarti veshalvi bal emot le'olam. A-donai birtzoncha he'emadta lehareri oz histarta fanecha hayiti nivhal. Elecha A-donai ekra ve'el A-donai etchanan. Ma betza bedami beridti el shachat hayodcha afar hayagid amitecha. Shema A-donai vechoneni A-donai heye ozer li. Hafachta mispedi lemachol li pitachta saki vate'azereni simcha. Lema'an yezamercha chavod velo yidom A-donai elohai le'olam odeka.

Meaning: A Psalm. A Song for the Dedication of the House. Of David. I will extol You, O Lord, for You have lifted me up, and have not let my enemies rejoice over me. O Lord my God, I cried out to You, and You healed me. O Lord, You brought up my soul from Sheol; You kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit. Sing to the Lord, O you His godly ones, and give thanks to His holy name. For His anger is but for a moment; His favor is for a lifetime. Weeping may tarry for the night, but joy comes with the morning. As for me, I said in my prosperity, “I shall never be moved.” By Your favor, O Lord, You made my mountain stand strong; You hid Your face; I was dismayed. To You, O Lord, I cry, and to the Lord I plead for mercy: “What profit is there in my death, if I go down to the pit? Will the dust praise You? Will it tell of Your faithfulness? Hear, O Lord, and be gracious to me! O Lord, be my helper!” You have turned for me my mourning into dancing; You have loosed my sackcloth and clothed me with gladness, that my glory may sing Your praise and not be silent. O Lord my God, I will give thanks to You forever!

Hanukkah starts on the 25th of Kislev in the Hebrew calendar, so its date in the regular calendar changes every year. Each candle is lit on the evening before that day of Hanukkah. Sunset and nightfall in your city: Zmanim for today.

How to light the Hanukkah menorah, step by step

1. Get the menorah ready

Set up the menorah with the number of candles or oil cups for tonight: one on the first night, two on the second, up to eight on the last night. The shamash, the helper candle, stands apart from the others, often higher. The menorah at the top of this page shows tonight’s count, and you can tap any night from 1 to 8 to see it.

2. Light the shamash and say the blessings

Light the shamash first. Holding it, say the two blessings, and on the first night the third blessing, Shehecheyanu, as well. On this page the first-night blessing is marked with a star, and on every other night it is shown faded so it is not said by mistake.

3. Light the candles and say Haneirot Halalu

Light the candles with the shamash. In the siddur used here, Haneirot Halalu (“We kindle these lights”) is said after the first candle is lit, while the rest are being lit. It reminds us that the Hanukkah lights are for looking at and giving thanks, not for using their light.

4. Sing Maoz Tzur

Many families sing Maoz Tzur next to the lit menorah. The tune most families know is the same around the world, and the transliteration under each line makes it easy for children and guests to sing along.

Questions about your own home

Where to put the menorah, which way to add the candles, what to do on Friday before Shabbat and how long the candles need to burn are matters of Jewish law with different customs between families. Ask your rabbi about your own home. For Friday, the time Shabbat begins in your city is on Shabbat candle lighting times.

What the Hanukkah blessings mean

Lehadlik ner chanuka

The first blessing thanks God, who “has sanctified us with His commandments, and commanded us to kindle the Hanukkah light”. Lighting the menorah is a mitzvah set by the Sages, and this blessing is said every night.

She’asa nisim

The second blessing thanks God “who performed miracles for our ancestors in those days, at this time”: the victory of the few Maccabees over the Greek army, and the small jug of pure oil that burned for eight days in the Temple.

Shehecheyanu

The third blessing, said on the first night only, thanks God for keeping us alive and bringing us to this season. The same blessing is said at the start of other festivals and on new things.

Questions and answers

What are the blessings for Hanukkah in Hebrew?

There are two blessings every night: "Baruch ata A-donai, eloheinu melech ha'olam, asher kideshanu bemitzvotav, vetzivanu lehadlik ner chanuka" (to kindle the Hanukkah light) and "Baruch ata A-donai, eloheinu melech ha'olam, she'asa nisim la'avoteinu bayamim hahem bazeman haze" (who performed miracles for our ancestors). On the first night a third blessing is added: Shehecheyanu. The full Hebrew with vowels, the transliteration and the English are at the top of this page.

What do you say when lighting Hanukkah candles?

The blessings are said before lighting. Then the candles are lit, and after the first candle is lit, Haneirot Halalu ("We kindle these lights") is said. Many families then sing Maoz Tzur. All of these are on this page in Hebrew, transliteration and English.

Which blessing is said only on the first night of Hanukkah?

Shehecheyanu, the blessing of thanks for reaching this season, is added on the first night only. The page marks it, and on the other nights it is shown faded so you do not say it by mistake.

What night of Hanukkah is it tonight?

The box at the top of the page works it out from your device's date and shows the menorah for tonight. Each night of Hanukkah begins in the evening, so the candles for a given night are lit on the evening before that day.

When is Hanukkah 2026?

In 2026 the first candle is lit on the evening of Friday, December 4, and the eighth candle on the evening of Friday, December 11. The table on this page lists the first night of Hanukkah for the next ten years.

How many candles do you light each night?

One candle on the first night, two on the second, and one more each night up to eight on the eighth night, plus the shamash, the helper candle used to light the others. The menorah drawing on this page shows tonight's count.

How do you say the Hanukkah blessings if you cannot read Hebrew?

Read the transliteration line under the Hebrew: it spells each Hebrew word in English letters. "ch" is said like the ch in the Scottish word loch, and an apostrophe marks a short break between two vowels.

What is Maoz Tzur?

Maoz Tzur ("Rock of my salvation") is a song sung after lighting the Hanukkah candles. Its stanzas tell of the Jewish people's rescue from Egypt, Babylon, Haman and the Greeks; the fifth stanza tells the story of Hanukkah and the oil. All six stanzas are on this page.
