A birth close to sunset

The Hebrew day begins at sunset, so a baby born after sunset counts as born on the next day. Between sunset and nightfall there is a doubt, and the calendar marks it. If you know the time of birth, choose "I know the time", and sunset is calculated for the city you chose.

Brit milah on Shabbat: the main rule

A brit on time, on the eighth day after a natural birth, is done even on Shabbat. A brit that was postponed, for example because the baby was unwell, no longer overrides Shabbat and moves to a weekday. A brit after a cesarean birth is also not done on Shabbat. And if the baby was born at twilight on Friday evening, there is a doubt whether he was born on Friday or on Shabbat, so the brit is usually on Sunday. The calculator marks each of these cases.

Preparing for a Shabbat brit

Everything that can be done before Shabbat is done before it: the tools are prepared and brought to the place of the brit in advance. The mohel and the family plan how to walk there, since there is no driving on Shabbat. Ask a rabbi about the details ahead of time.

Yom Kippur and fast days

A brit on time is held on Yom Kippur, on Tisha B'Av and on other fasts. These days have their own customs, for example about the cup of wine and the festive meal, so ask a rabbi about the order of the ceremony in advance.

When the brit is postponed

The brit is postponed when the baby is not well, for example with strong jaundice. The mohel and the doctor decide, and a rabbi in case of doubt. Once the baby is well a new date is set, and that brit is not done on Shabbat or a festival.

Questions and answers

Is a brit that falls on Shabbat postponed?

A brit at its proper time overrides Shabbat, but there are cases where it is postponed, such as a birth by cesarean section or at twilight. The calendar shows each case with its source, and in a doubt you ask the rabbi.

What decides whether the baby was born by day or by night?

Sunset and nightfall. Choose the time of birth and the city, and the calculator checks.

Is there a difference in the diaspora?

Yes, because of the second festival day. Choose "Diaspora".

What happens when the eighth day falls on Shabbat?

A brit at its proper time, on the eighth day, is performed on Shabbat too. A postponed brit, and a brit after a cesarean section, do not override Shabbat. The calendar marks each case with the laws in brief, and in a practical case you ask the rabbi.
