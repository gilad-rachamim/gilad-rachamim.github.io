What the parents see

A name, a qualification, an area and liturgical traditions, and a personal line. And buttons: call, write on WhatsApp, and save you in their contacts with one tap. So parents who received the card from a friend can find you months later too.

A link or an image

The link suits a signature, a bio and messages. The QR image suits a status, groups, and printing on an A5 sheet to put up in the synagogue. Choose one of five designs and a size: status, square or a sheet for printing.

Updating your details

Did your details change? Fill the form in again and create a new card. Whoever prefers not to store the details on a server can choose a long link, with all the details written inside it. The card works on any phone, with no app.

Why it is free

The tools for mohels are built as an act of kindness, for rabbis, gabbaim, synagogues and the whole family: with no payment, no ads and no sign-up. If you want to help, send the page to someone it could help.

What to put on the card

Full name, a phone number with a WhatsApp button, your area of work and your qualification. Parents save the card to their contacts with one tap, and it can be passed on to friends who are looking for a mohel. For the brit details form: the brit details form.

Updating without printing

You change a number or an area, and the link stays the same.

How parents find a mohel

Most parents find a mohel through a recommendation from friends, family or the synagogue. When satisfied parents pass on a digital card, and not just a phone number, the recipient sees the name, the area and the traditions at once, and saves the details with one tap. So the recommendation does not get lost among the messages.

Ideas for the personal line

The personal line is the place to say in one sentence what matters to you that parents know. For example: that you are available for questions before and after the brit, the areas where you work, or the languages in which you speak with families. It is best to write a short, warm sentence, and leave the long details for the conversation with the parents.

A photo or a logo

A clear photo of your face on a clean background helps parents recognise you when you arrive at the brit. Whoever prefers no photo can upload a logo.

Send the card after the brit

After the brit many parents want to say thank you and recommend you to friends. Send them the link to the card together with your congratulations. That way they have something organised to pass on, and friends who are expecting a baby get your details clearly, with the phone and the area, in one tap.

Questions and answers

How does a mohel get a digital business card?

Fill in the details and press "Create my card". You get a link at once.

Can parents save me in their contacts?

Yes, with one tap from the card.

Is there a printable version?

Yes. An image with a QR code on an A5 sheet, or for status and WhatsApp.

Are my details stored?

With the short link the card is stored only so that the link can open it, and you can ask for it to be deleted. There is also a long link with nothing stored.

Can I print the card?

Yes. Choose the size "A5 sheet for printing", with a QR code that leads to the card.

Is this page free?

Yes. Everything here is free, with no sign-up and no ads.
