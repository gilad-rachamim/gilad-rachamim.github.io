    Brit milah log for mohels, on your phone

    Record each brit in a minute: the parents' names, date and time, place, sandak, the baby's name and phone. Tick that you have seen the baby and removed the dressing the next day, and see at any moment what is still to do.

    Pidyon haben and files

    For a mother's firstborn, the log reminds you of the date of the pidyon haben. Download to Excel or Word, print, and back up to a file. If you prefer to work without the site, the page also has a Word file to fill in and a WhatsApp template.

    The log is kept only on your phone, and no detail is sent anywhere. It was built at the request of a mohel from the mohels' forum. More for mohels: the brit details form and the special brit calendar.

    Tracking each brit

    Each brit has two ticks: "Saw the baby" and "Removed the dressing the next day". So you can see at any moment what is still open. You can also record the sandak, the baby's name, the parents' phone and notes.

    When is the pidyon haben

    For a mother's firstborn, tick the box and the log reminds you of the date of the pidyon haben, on day 31 after birth. If you did not enter a birth date, the birth date is taken as seven days before the brit.

    Backup and moving to a new phone

    The log is kept only on the phone. Download it to Excel or Word, print or save a PDF, and keep a backup file. When you change phones, load the backup and everything is back.

    How much does it cost?

    Nothing. The tool is built as an act of kindness, with no ads and no collecting of details. Use it as much as you like, and send it to the gabbai, the rabbi or friends.

    At the end of the year

    You can see how many brits there were in each month.

    Why a mohel keeps a brit milah log

    A mohel who does many brits needs to remember who he visited, when to call the parents again and which family has a pidyon haben coming up. The log keeps all of this in one list on the phone, and at the end of the year it gives a clear summary of every brit.

    Private by design

    Nothing in the log is sent to a server or shared with anyone. It stays on your phone until you export it.

    Questions and answers

    How does a mohel keep track of his brits?

    Record each brit in the log, tick what was done, and see in the list what is left.

    Are the details sent to anyone?

    No. The log is kept only on the phone. Back it up to a file from time to time so as not to lose it.

    Can I export to Excel?

    Yes, and also to Word or PDF.

    Is there a reminder for the pidyon haben?

    Yes. Tick "mother's firstborn" and the log works out day 31 after birth.

    Does the log remind me of the pidyon haben?

    Yes. For a mother's firstborn, tick the option and the log shows the date of the pidyon haben, on day 31 after birth.

    Is this page free?

    Yes. Everything here is free, with no sign-up and no ads.

    Does it work on a phone too?

    Yes. The page opens in the browser of a phone or a computer, and nothing needs to be installed.
