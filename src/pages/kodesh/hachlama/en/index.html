Ready messages for the mohel, from the first call to after the brit

A kit of WhatsApp messages to the parents, each one on time: after the first call, the day before the brit, the evening before, and up to a week after. Each message has the instructions for that day, for example what to buy and what to bring.

Fill in once

Your name and phone are saved for next time. Add the family's details and the date of the brit, and next to each message it says when to send it. With the parents' phone, the button opens the chat directly. You can change any text.

The messages are a working kit for the mohel, and each mohel adapts them to their own instructions. More for mohels: the brit details form and all the tools for mohels.

The messages in order

Each message arrives at its own time: after the first call, before the brit, the evening before, and in the days after. Next to each one it says when to send it, and the "Go to today's message" button jumps to the one that fits the date. That way the parents get the right instruction exactly when they need it, and not everything on one day.

Change it to your own style

You can edit any message before sending, so it sounds the way you talk. If you changed one and want to go back, "Original text" restores the ready-made version. Every message shows your name and phone, and the baby's name after the brit.

Start a kit for the next family

"New family" clears the parents' details and starts a new kit, while your own name and phone stay saved. With the parents' phone, each message opens directly in their WhatsApp, ready to send.

Why it is free

The Jewish tools are built as an act of kindness, for rabbis, synagogue wardens, synagogues and every family: with no payment, no ads and no sign-up. If you want to help, send this page to someone it can help.

Questions and answers

What do you send the parents after a brit milah?

The kit has a message for every day, with the instructions. Each mohel adapts the text to their own instructions.

Can I change the text?

Yes. Edit any message, and there is a button to go back to the original text.

How do I know when to send each message?

Fill in the date of the brit, and next to each message it says when.

Is it free?

Yes, free and with no sign-up.

When do I send each message?

Next to each message it says when to send it, based on the date of the brit. "Go to today's message" shows the one that fits today.

Is the family's information saved?

The family's details are kept only on your phone and are cleared after about a month. Your own name and phone stay for next time.
