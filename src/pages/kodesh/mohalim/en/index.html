Mohel Tools: Free Brit Milah Tools for Mohels and Families

Dear colleagues, I gathered all the tools here for you, to use with joy and love. Use them as much as you need, and send them to the families you work with too. Even if I am not the mohel, that is perfectly fine.

How it works

Every tool opens on a phone, with no install and no sign-up. Each tool sits on my website, so my details appear in each of them. Your own data (the log, drafts) stays only on your phone.

For you, the mohels

My brit milah log

Record each brit in a minute from your phone: parents, Hebrew and civil date, time and place. Mark that you saw the baby and removed the dressing, and get a "what is left to do" list, reminders and a file for Excel and Word. Everything is stored only on your device.

No website? There is also a Word file to fill in.

Brit details form

Send parents a personal link, and they fill in on their phone the birth details, weight, jaundice, place and time of the brit, liturgical tradition and sandak. The form calculates the brit date, and everything reaches you in one tidy WhatsApp message. One tap in the message adds the brit to your log.

Digital business card

Fill in your name, phone, area and nusach and get your own card: parents save you in their contacts, write on WhatsApp or call with one tap. The details sit in the link itself and are not stored with me.

No website? There is also a QR image for your status, WhatsApp and printing.

Brit honors card

Choose the honors by custom, and your name and phone appear at the bottom of the card. Send parents a link, they fill in the names of those honored and get an image for WhatsApp and a PDF to print. It also has a "what to bring" list, a schedule and the naming text.

Free tools for mohels

Here are working tools for a mohel, in English: a brit milah log, a brit details form for parents, a digital business card, honors cards, aftercare messages for parents and a calendar of special brit dates. Everything runs in the phone browser, with no app and no sign-up.

Who made this

The tools were built by Gilad Rachamim, a certified mohel, as an act of kindness (chesed): free, with no ads and not for profit.

If parents do not use websites

The without a website page has WhatsApp templates in English: the preparation list, the brit date, the honors, the brit day and the aftercare instructions. You copy them, fill them in and send.

Questions and answers

How much do the tools cost?

Nothing. All the tools are free, with no ads and no limits.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open the page on your phone and start using it.

Where is my data stored?

The brit log and drafts are stored only on your phone. Each tool says on its page exactly what is saved.

Can I send the tools to parents if I am not the mohel?

Yes, of course. Use them and send them to the families you work with as much as you need.

Are all the tools in English?

The tools for the mohel, yes. Some pages for parents are still in Hebrew, and we keep translating.

Is there a printable or Word version?

Yes. The log, the form and the aftercare messages each have a Word file in English, and the without-a-website page has ready WhatsApp templates.

How do I report a translation mistake?

Press "Send me a correction or a note" and WhatsApp opens. I am grateful for every correction.
