Brit Milah Shopping List: What to Buy Before

Toward the end of pregnancy, when the belly starts to feel heavy, you never know exactly when the birth will come. A strong urge to nest wakes up inside us, so that everything is ready for the birth of the son.

עבריתEnglishРусский

I recommend doing the shopping already at this stage. These things do not spoil, and after the birth you are always busy – within eight days you are putting on a whole event. That said, you do not have to buy everything: some things may arrive as gifts. I have prepared an organized list of the essentials for you.

I am Gilad Rachamim, a certified mohel. You can tick off the same list in the preparation checklist on your phone – free, no sign-up: your ticks are saved only on your device, and you can share the list with your spouse.

Pharmacy, for after the bris

  • Esracaine 5% to numb the baby's pain after the bris
  • Alcohol wipes, or 70% alcohol and cotton wool to clean the baby's umbilical cord stump
  • Novimol in case of pain after the bris, and because it is always good to have a pain reliever for the baby at home
  • Nursing cream to ease soreness
  • Natural oil (almond or paraffin) to apply after the bris, to prevent rubbing that could hurt the baby
  • Bepanthen Plus or mupirocin to apply after the bandage is removed

Esracaine and Novimol are Israeli brand names.

A mohel's tip

Buy these items before the birth. After the bris you do not want to be running to a pharmacy. In our conversation before the bris we will go over them together. And how do you use all this after the bris? The day-by-day instructions.

How the pharmacy items are used after the bris

  • The oil is dripped onto the bandage after every diaper change – on the day of the bris and the next day, until the mohel removes the bandage. If you have no almond or paraffin oil, a good olive oil will do.
  • Novimol – following the mohel's instructions, when the baby is unsettled: the syrup may be given every five or six hours, at the dose on the package.
  • Bepanthen Plus or mupirocin goes around the area after the bandage comes off – twice a day, morning and night, for three days. No more than that: if it seems more is needed, only in coordination with the mohel.

All the details are on the bris aftercare page, and what really calms the baby is on the page about pain and soothing.

Hygiene for mother and baby

  • Newborn-size diapers, wipes
  • A changing mat so the bedding does not get dirty. A changing table is not a must, but a mat is. There are handy mats that fold into a bag and are really cheap
  • Baby shampoo so it does not sting

First clothes

  • Clothes for the baby for the first days, until the bris. At the bris you will see what you receive, and then fill in the rest
  • Cloth diapers for spit-up, and for wrapping the baby in a "mohel's swaddle" to soothe the pain

Clothes for the bris

  • Festive clothes for the bris for the whole family. A bris is a festive, joyful occasion – a great privilege that new life has come into the world. And it is not only the baby's event, but a meaningful event for you, the parents who have become a family
  • A bris outfit for the baby. It is important that it has separate pants or leggings and a top, so that during the bris the baby keeps the top on and is not fully exposed

Safe sleep and getting around

  • An infant car seat is a must, so you can leave the hospital safely. You can buy a car seat that clicks onto a wheeled base or a stroller, so if the baby is asleep you do not need to move him, and you can keep carrying him easily
  • A crib. For safety reasons it is not recommended to leave the baby sleeping in the stroller for long
  • A stroller. If you think someone may offer to buy you one, it is worth waiting. If not, go ahead and buy. Make sure the stroller suits your height

Toys for the baby

For now you can skip them. At the bris you will see what you receive, and afterward fill in what is needed.

What to bring on the day of the brit milah

Besides the shopping, there are things needed at the ceremony itself. Get them ready the evening before:

  • A bris pillow, a kiddush cup and dry red wine, two tallitot (for the father and the sandek).
  • Besamim – by Sephardic and Mizrahi custom, myrtle is preferred; a siddur for the blessings, unless the mohel brings one.
  • A diaper bag: cloth diapers, disposable diapers, wipes, a change of clothes, formula (if he is not breastfed), a pacifier and diaper cream. Plus sugar water and a clean pacifier for soothing.

The full list, and how to get the baby ready, is in the chapter on the day of the bris.

Brit milah shopping: common questions

What do I buy at the pharmacy before a brit milah?

Esracaine 5%, Novimol, natural oil (almond or paraffin), Bepanthen Plus or mupirocin, and alcohol wipes for cleaning the umbilical stump. Buy them all before the birth, so you will not need to run to a pharmacy after the bris.

When is the best time to shop?

While you are still pregnant. These things do not spoil, and after the birth you are always busy: within eight days you are putting on an event.

What does not need to be bought before the birth?

Toys, and most clothes beyond the first days. At the bris you will see what you received as gifts, and afterward you can fill in what is missing.

Why is oil needed after the bris?

It is dripped onto the bandage after every diaper change. A soft, oiled bandage does not rub and comes off easily later, while a dried bandage stuck to the wound hurts when removed.

What should the baby wear to the bris?

A two-piece outfit: separate pants or leggings and a top. During the bris the baby keeps the top on, so he is not fully exposed and does not get cold.

Do we need a car seat to leave the hospital?

Yes, a car seat is a must to leave the hospital safely. A model that clicks onto a stroller is handy – then you do not need to move a sleeping baby.

Is a changing table necessary?

No. A changing mat is, so the bedding does not get dirty. There are handy mats that fold into a bag and cost very little.

Is this list available on my phone?

Yes. In the preparation checklist you can tick off what you have bought, and the list is saved on your phone – free, no sign-up. The whole guide is also available as the guide for parents.
