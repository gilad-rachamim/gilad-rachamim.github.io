Why prepare early

Toward the end of pregnancy you never know exactly when the birth will come. Nothing on the list spoils, and after the birth you are always busy: within eight days you are hosting a celebration. That is why the first part of the guide is what to buy now, so that after the birth you are free for the baby, not for shopping. To tick items off on your phone right away, open the brit milah checklist.

When the brit will be

A brit milah takes place on the eighth day after birth. If the baby was born before sunset, the day of birth counts as day one; if after sunset, counting starts the next day. A brit on its proper day is held even on Shabbat and festivals, while a postponed brit is held only on a weekday. The brit milah date calculator works out the date for you.

The jaundice test

A jaundice test is a must. Tell the mohel the result and the day and time the test was taken, because it makes a difference. If the level is borderline or high, you will be asked to do more tests. The baby's health comes first: the brit is held only once he is fit for it, and the mohel decides the timing together with you.

The eight days, day by day

The day of birth: mazal tov, take a breath, and note whether the birth was before or after sunset. Then book the mohel and the place: one short call, and the earlier, the calmer. The jaundice test, at the hospital or the well-baby clinic (Tipat Halav): keep the result, the day and the time. Honors: sandek, kvatter, who says the blessings – check in the family who is customarily given which. A check before the brit: you send a photo and the hospital discharge letter. Last shopping from the list. On the evening before, the Brit Yitzchak: learning, Psalms and singing around the baby. And the day of the brit: feed the baby until about an hour before, and arrive in a good mood.

How to choose a mohel

The guide gives four points. The baby's health: a mohel who studied milah properly and gives precise aftercare instructions. Availability: a mohel who is with you from the first call, answers on WhatsApp, and comes to your home after the brit to remove the bandage. Atmosphere: songs and warm words that bring the guests in. And your peace of mind as parents, all the way to full healing. The easiest time to reach out is during pregnancy, with no pressure.
