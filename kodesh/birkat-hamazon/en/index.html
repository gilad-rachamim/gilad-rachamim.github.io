Rite: Edot HaMizrach

Zimun – the invitation to bless

When three or more ate bread together, one leads and the others answer

English: Sefaria Community Translation (CC0), from Sefaria, with the footnotes left out. The transliteration is a reading aid prepared for this page.

אם המסובים שלושה או יותר אומרים

If three or more ate together, say:

יאמר המזמן

The leader says:

הַב לָן וְנִבְרִיךְ לְמַלְכָּא עִלָּאָה קַדִּישָׁא:

Hav lan venivrich lemalka ila'a kadisha.

והמסובים עונים

The others answer:

שָׁמַיִם:

Shamayim.

ואומר המזמן

The leader says:

בִּרְשׁוּת מַלְכָּא עִלָּאָה קַדִּישָׁא (בשבת וּבִרְשׁוּת שַׁבָּת מַלְכְּתָא.) (ביו"ט וּבִרְשׁוּת יוֹמָא טָבָא אוּשְׁפִּיזָא קַדִּישָׁא.) (בסוכה וּבִרְשׁוּת שִׁבְעָה אוּשְׁפִּיזִין עִלָּאִין קַדִּישִׁין) וּבִרְשׁוּת מוֹרַי וְרַבּוֹתַי וּבִרְשׁוּתְכֶם. נְבָרֵךְ (בעשרה ויותר: אֱלֹקֵינוּ) שֶׁאָכַלְנוּ מִשֶּׁלּוֹ:

Birshut malka ila'a kadisha (On Shabbat: uvirshut shabat malketa.) (On a festival (Yom Tov): uvirshut yoma tava ushpiza kadisha.) (In the sukkah: uvirshut shiv'a ushpizin ila'in kadishin) uvirshut morai verabotai uvirshutchem. Nevarech (with ten or more: E-loheinu) she'achalnu mishelo.

והמסובים עונים

The others answer:

בָּרוּךְ (בעשרה ויותר: אֱלֹקֵינוּ) שֶׁאָכַלְנוּ מִשֶּׁלּוֹ וּבְטוּבוֹ חָיִינוּ:

Baruch (with ten or more: E-loheinu) she'achalnu mishelo uvtuvo chayinu.

והמזמן עונה

The leader answers:

בָּרוּךְ (בעשרה ויותר: אֱלֹקֵינוּ) שֶׁאָכַלְנוּ מִשֶּׁלּוֹ וּבְטוּבוֹ חָיִינוּ:

Baruch (with ten or more: E-loheinu) she'achalnu mishelo uvtuvo chayinu.

המזמן אומר:

The leader says:

רַבּוֹתַי נְבָרֵךְ:

Rabotai nevarech.

"Gentlemen, let us say the blessing:

המסובים עונים:

The others answer:

יְהִי שֵׁם ה' מְבֹרָךְ מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם:

Yehi shem A-donai mevorach me'ata ve'ad olam.

The Name of Adonoy will be blessed from now forever.

המזמן חוזר:

The leader repeats:

יְהִי שֵׁם ה' מְבֹרָךְ מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם:

Yehi shem A-donai mevorach me'ata ve'ad olam.

"The Name of Adonoy will be blessed from now forever."

וממשיך:

And continues:

בִּרְשׁוּת מָרָנָן וְרַבָּנָן וְרַבּוֹתַי נְבָרֵךְ (בעשרה אֱלֹקֵינוּ) שֶׁאָכַלְנוּ מִשֶּׁלּוֹ:

Birshut maranan verabanan verabotai nevarech (with ten: E-loheinu) she'achalnu mishelo.

"With your permission our masters and teachers, Let us bless (our God,) Him, for we have eaten of His bounty."

The others answer:

בָּרוּךְ (בעשרה אֱלֹקֵינוּ) שֶׁאָכַלְנוּ מִשֶּׁלּוֹ וּבְטוּבוֹ חָיִינוּ:

Baruch (with ten: E-loheinu) she'achalnu mishelo uvtuvo chayinu.

Blessed is (our God) He for we have eaten of His bounty and through His goodness we live.

Before Birkat HaMazon: mayim acharonim, Lamnatze'ach and Avarecha

יטול מים אחרונים, ויאמר

After mayim acharonim (water on the fingertips), say:

לַמְנַצֵּחַ בִּנְגִינֹת מִזְמוֹר שִׁיר: אֱלֹקִים יְחָנֵּנוּ וִיבָרְכֵנוּ יָאֵר פָּנָיו אִתָּנוּ סֶלָה: לָדַעַת בָּאָרֶץ דַּרְכֶּךָ בְּכָל־גּוֹיִם יְשׁוּעָתֶךָ: יוֹדוּךָ עַמִּים אֱלֹקִים יוֹדוּךָ עַמִּים כֻּלָּם: יִשְׂמְחוּ וִירַנְּנוּ לְאֻמִּים כִּי־תִשְׁפֹּט עַמִּים מִישֹׁר וּלְאֻמִּים בָּאָרֶץ תַּנְחֵם סֶלָה: יוֹדוּךָ עַמִּים אֱלֹקִים יוֹדוּךָ עַמִּים כֻּלָּם: אֶרֶץ נָתְנָה יְבוּלָהּ יְבָרְכֵנוּ אֱלֹקִים אֱלֹקֵינוּ: יְבָרְכֵנוּ אֱלֹקִים וְיִירְאוּ אוֹתוֹ כָּל־אַפְסֵי־אָרֶץ:

Lamnatzeach binginot mizmor shir. E-lohim yechanenu vivarchenu ya'er panav itanu sela. Lada'at ba'aretz darkecha bechol goyim yeshu'atecha. Yoducha amim E-lohim yoducha amim kulam. Yismechu viranenu le'umim ki tishpot amim mishor ul'umim ba'aretz tanchem sela. Yoducha amim E-lohim yoducha amim kulam. Eretz natna yevulah yevarchenu E-lohim E-loheinu. Yevarchenu E-lohim veyir'u oto kol afsei aretz.

אֲבָרְכָה אֶת־ה' בְּכָל־עֵת תָּמִיד תְּהִלָּתוֹ בְּפִי: סוֹף דָּבָר הַכֹּל נִשְׁמָע אֶת־הָאֱלֹקִים יְרָא וְאֶת־מִצְוֹתָיו שְׁמוֹר כִּי־זֶה כָּל־הָאָדָם: תְּהִלַּת ה' יְדַבֶּר פִּי וִיבָרֵךְ כָּל־בָּשָׂר שֵׁם קָדְשׁוֹ לְעוֹלָם וָעֶד: וַאֲנַחְנוּ נְבָרֵךְ יָהּ מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם הַלְלוּיָהּ: וַיְדַבֵּר אֵלַי זֶה הַשֻּׁלְחָן אֲשֶׁר לִפְנֵי ה':

Avarcha et A-donai bechol et tamid tehilato befi. Sof davar hakol nishma et ha'E-lohim yera ve'et mitzvotav shemor ki ze kol ha'adam. Tehilat A-donai yedaber pi vivarech kol basar shem kodsho le'olam va'ed. Va'anachnu nevarech yah me'ata ve'ad olam haleluyah. Vayedaber elai ze hashulchan asher lifnei A-donai.

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה', אֱלֹקֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, הָאֵל הַזָּן אוֹתָנוּ וְאֶת־הָעוֹלָם כֻּלּוֹ בְּטוּבוֹ, בְּחֵן בְּחֶסֶד בְּרֶוַח וּבְרַחֲמִים רַבִּים, נֹתֵן לֶחֶם לְכָל־בָּשָׂר כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ. וּבְטוּבוֹ הַגָּדוֹל תָּמִיד לֹא חָסַר לָנוּ, וְאַל־יֶחְסַר לָנוּ מָזוֹן תָּמִיד לְעוֹלָם וָעֶד, כִּי הוּא אֵל זָן וּמְפַרְנֵס לַכֹּל וְשֻׁלְחָנוֹ עָרוּךְ לַכֹּל, וְהִתְקִין מִחְיָה וּמָזוֹן לְכָל־בְּרִיּוֹתָיו אֲשֶׁר בָּרָא בְּרַחֲמָיו וּבְרוֹב חֲסָדָיו. כָּאָמוּר, פּוֹתֵחַ אֶת־יָדֶךָ (ר"ת פא"י שהוא מספר יאהדונהי ומספר סא"ל וס"ת חת"ך) וּמַשְׂבִּיעַ (כמספר חת"ך) לְכָל־חַי רָצוֹן. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה', הַזָּן אֶת־הַכֹּל:

Baruch ata A-donai, E-loheinu melech ha'olam, ha'el hazan otanu ve'et ha'olam kulo betuvo, bechen bechesed berevach uvrachamim rabim, noten lechem lechol basar ki le'olam chasdo. Uvtuvo hagadol tamid lo chasar lanu, ve'al yechsar lanu mazon tamid le'olam va'ed, ki hu el zan umefarnes lakol veshulchano aruch lakol, vehitkin michya umazon lechol beriyotav asher bara berachamav uvrov chasadav. Ka'amur, poteach et yadecha (a note in the siddur on the initial and final letters of these words) umasbia (a note in the siddur on the numerical value) lechol chai ratzon. Baruch ata A-donai, hazan et hakol.

Blessed are You, L·rd, our G·d, King of the universe. The G·d, Who feeds us & the entire world, with His Goodness, with Grace, with Kindness, with Abundance, & with great Mercy. He Provides bread to all flesh – because forever is His kindness. (Psalms 136, 25) And with His great goodness, we continually never lack. And May You never let us lack food continually forever & ever. For He is G·d, Who feeds & provides livelihood to everyone. And His table is set for all. The Merciful One, He will bequeath to us a world that is complete rest and serenity for eternity

He who gives great deliverance to His king, (On Shabbos and Yom Tov say: He who is a tower of deliverance to His king,) and shows kindness to His anointed— to David and his descendants forever. He Who makes peace in His high heavens may He make peace for us and for all Israel, and say, Amein.

Yer'u et A-donai kedoshav, ki ein machsor lire'av. Kefirim rashu vera'evu, vedorshei A-donai lo yachseru chol tov. Hodu lA-donai ki tov, ki le'olam chasdo. Fear Adonoy, [you] His holy ones, for those who fear Him suffer no deprivation. Young lions may feel want and hunger, but those who seek Adonoy, will not be deprived of any good thing. Give thanks to Adonoy, for He is good, for His kindness endures forever. You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living being. Blessed is the man who trusts in Adonoy, so that Adonoy is his security. I was young and I have grown old, yet I have never seen a righteous man forsaken, nor his children begging for bread. Adonoy will give strength to His people, Adonoy will bless His people with peace.

Before Birkat HaMazon: Al Naharot Bavel (weekdays) and Shir HaMa'alot (Shabbat and festivals)

בחול קודם ברכת המזון אומרים:

On weekdays, before Birkat HaMazon, say:

עַל־נַהֲרוֹת בָּבֶל שָׁם יָשַׁבְנוּ גַּם־בָּכִינוּ בְּזָכְרֵנוּ אֶת־צִיּוֹן: עַל־עֲרָבִים בְּתוֹכָהּ תָּלִינוּ כִּנֹּרוֹתֵינוּ: כִּי שָׁם שְׁאֵלוּנוּ שׁוֹבֵינוּ דִּבְרֵי־שִׁיר וְתוֹלָלֵינוּ שִׂמְחָה שִׁירוּ לָנוּ מִשִּׁיר צִיּוֹן: אֵיךְ נָשִׁיר אֶת־שִׁיר־ה' עַל אַדְמַת נֵכָר: אִם־אֶשְׁכָּחֵךְ יְרוּשָׁלָםִ תִּשְׁכַּח יְמִינִי: תִּדְבַּק לְשׁוֹנִי לְחִכִּי אִם־לֹא אֶזְכְּרֵכִי אִם־לֹא אַעֲלֶה אֶת־יְרוּשָׁלַםִ עַל רֹאשׁ שִׂמְחָתִי: זְכֹר ה' לִבְנֵי אֱדוֹם אֵת יוֹם יְרוּשָׁלָםִ הָאֹמְרִים עָרוּ עָרוּ עַד הַיְסוֹד בָּהּ: בַּת־בָּבֶל הַשְּׁדוּדָה אַשְׁרֵי שֶׁיְּשַׁלֶּם־לָךְ אֶת־גְּמוּלֵךְ שֶׁגָּמַלְתְּ לָנוּ: אַשְׁרֵי שֶׁיֹּאחֵז וְנִפֵּץ אֶת־עֹלָלַיִךְ אֶל־הַסָּלַע:

Al naharot bavel sham yashavnu gam bachinu bezochrenu et tziyon. Al aravim betochah talinu kinoroteinu. Ki sham she'elunu shoveinu divrei shir vetolaleinu simcha shiru lanu mishir tziyon. Eich nashir et shir A-donai al admat nechar. Im eshkachech yerushalayim tishkach yemini. Tidbak leshoni lechiki im lo ezkerechi im lo a'ale et yerushalayim al rosh simchati. Zechor A-donai livnei edom et yom yerushalayim ha'omrim aru aru ad hayesod bah. Bat bavel hasheduda ashrei sheyeshalem lach et gemulech shegamalt lanu. Ashrei sheyochez venipetz et olalayich el hasala.

By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat and we also wept, when we remembered Zion. Upon the willows in its midst, we hung our harps. For there our captors demanded of us words of song, and our tormentors asked of us [with] mirth; Sing to us from the song[s] of Zion. How shall we sing the song of Adonoy on alien soil? If I ever forget you, Jerusalem, may my right hand forget [its movement]. May my tongue cleave to my palate, if I remember you not; if I set not Jerusalem above my highest joy. Remember, Adonoy, to the sons of Edom the day of Jerusalem, [it was they] who said, Raze it, raze it to its very foundation. Daughter of Babylon, you are the annihilated one; fortunate is he who will repay you for all you have done to us. Fortunate is he who will take and dash your little ones against the rock.

בשבת קודם ברכת המזון אומרים:

On Shabbat, before Birkat HaMazon, say:

שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת בְּשׁוּב ה' אֶת־שִׁיבַת צִיּוֹן הָיִינוּ כְּחֹלְמִים: אָז יִמָּלֵא שְׂחוֹק פִּינוּ וּלְשׁוֹנֵנוּ רִנָּה, אָז יֹאמְרוּ בַגּוֹיִם הִגְדִּיל ה' לַעֲשׂוֹת עִם־אֵלֶּה: הִגְדִּיל ה' לַעֲשׂוֹת עִמָּנוּ, הָיִינוּ שְׂמֵחִים: שׁוּבָה ה' אֶת־שְׁבִיתֵנוּ כַּאֲפִיקִים בַּנֶּגֶב: הַזֹּרְעִים בְּדִמְעָה, בְּרִנָּה יִקְצֹרוּ: הָלוֹךְ יֵלֵךְ וּבָכֹה, נֹשֵׂא מֶשֶׁךְ־הַזָּרַע, בֹּא־יָבֹא בְרִנָּה, נֹשֵׂא אֲלֻמֹּתָיו:

Shir hama'alot beshuv A-donai et shivat tziyon hayinu kecholmim. Az yimale sechok pinu ulshonenu rina, az yomru vagoyim higdil A-donai la'asot im ele. Higdil A-donai la'asot imanu, hayinu semechim. Shuva A-donai et shevitenu ka'afikim banegev. Hazor'im bedim'a, berina yiktzoru. Haloch yelech uvacho, nose meshech hazara, bo yavo verina, nose alumotav.

A Song of Ascents. When Adonoy brings about the return to Zion we will have been like dreamers. Then will our mouths be filled with laughter, and our tongue with joyous song. Then will they say among the nations: ‘Adonoy has done great things for them.’ Adonoy had done great things for us; we will [then] rejoice. Adonoy! bring back our exiles like springs in the desert. Those who sow in tears will reap with joyous song. [Though] he walks along weeping, carrying the bag of seed, he will return with joyous song carrying his sheaves.

On Chanukah and Purim: Al HaNisim

בחנוכה ופורים אומרים כאן על הניסים. ואם לא אמרו אין מחזירין אותו. ומכל מקום יוכל לאמרו בתוך שאר הרחמן. ויאמר הָרַחֲמָן הוּא יַעֲשֶׂה־לָּנוּ נִסִים וְנִפְלָאוֹת כְּמוֹ שֶׁעָשָׂה לַאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בַּיָּמִים הָהֵם בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה וכו' בִּימֵי מַתִּתְיָהוּ וכו' או בִּימֵי מָרְדְּכַי וכו'.

[For someone who did not say Al HaNisim on Chanukah or Purim – the line said among the HaRachaman requests:] harachaman hu ya'ase lanu nisim venifla'ot kemo she'asa la'avoteinu bayamim hahem bazeman haze [etc.] bimei matityahu [etc., or] bimei mordechai [etc.]

On regular weekdays, continue, “For everything”. On Chanukah and Purim add עַל הַנִּסִּים. If you forgot to say עַל הַנִּסִּים, you may include it when you reach הָרַחֲמָן by saying: הָרַחֲמָן הוּא יַעֲשֶׂה־לָּנוּ נִסִים וְנִפְלָאוֹת כְּמוֹ שֶׁעָשָׂה לַאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בַּיָּמִים הָהֵם בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה. and then continue on Chanukah with: בִּימֵי מַתִּתְיָהוּ, and on Purim with: בִּימֵי מָרְדְּכַי וְאֶסְתֵּר. If you reminded yourself before concluding the second blessing עַל הָאָרֶץ וְעַל הַמָּזוֹן, you may repeat it at that time and continue with the Birkas Hamazon.

וְעַל הַנִּסִּים וְעַל הַפֻּרְקָן וְעַל הַגְּבוּרוֹת וְעַל הַתְּשׁוּעוֹת וְעַל הַמִּלְחָמוֹת שֶׁעָשִׂיתָ לַאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בַּיָּמִים הָהֵם בִּזְּמַן הַזֶּה:

Ve'al hanisim ve'al hapurkan ve'al hagevurot ve'al hateshu'ot ve'al hamilchamot she'asita la'avoteinu bayamim hahem bizeman haze.

[We thank You] for the miracles for the redemption, for the mighty deeds, for the deliverances, for the wonders for the consolations and for the wars that You performed for our fathers in those days at this season.

בחנוכה אומרים:

On Chanukah say:

בִּימֵי מַתִּתְיָהוּ בֶּן־יוֹחָנָן כֹּהֵן גָּדוֹל חַשְׁמוֹנַאי וּבָנָיו כְּשֶׁעָמְדָה מַלְכוּת יָוָן הָרְשָׁעָה עַל־עַמְּךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל לְהַשְׁכִּיחָם תּוֹרָתֶךָ וּלְהַעֲבִירָם מֵחֻקֵּי רְצוֹנֶךָ: וְאַתָּה בְּרַחֲמֶיךָ הָרַבִּים עָמַדְתָּ לָהֶם בְּעֵת צָרָתָם רַבְתָּ אֶת־רִיבָם דַּנְתָּ אֶת־דִּינָם נָקַמְתָּ אֶת־נִקְמָתָם מָסַרְתָּ גִבּוֹרִים בְּיַד חַלָּשִׁים וְרַבִּים בְּיַד מְעַטִּים וּטְמֵאִים בְּיַד טְהוֹרִים וּרְשָׁעִים בְּיַד צַדִּיקִים וְזֵדִים בְּיַד עוֹסְקֵי תוֹרָתֶךָ וּלְךָ עָשִׂיתָ שֵׁם גָּדוֹל וְקָדוֹשׁ בְּעוֹלָמֶךָ וּלְעַמְּךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל עָשִׂיתָ תְּשׁוּעָה גְדוֹלָה וּפֻרְקָן כְּהַיּוֹם הַזֶּה וְאַחַר כַּךְ בָּאוּ בָנֶיךָ לִדְבִיר בֵּיתֶךָ וּפִנּוּ אֶת הֵיכָלֶךָ וְטִהֲרוּ אֶת מִקְדָּשֶׁךָ וְהִדְלִיקוּ נֵרוֹת בְּחַצְרוֹת קָדְשֶׁךָ וְקָבְעוּ שְׁמוֹנַת יְמֵי חֲנֻכָּה אֵלּוּ לְהוֹדוֹת וּלְהַלֵּל לְשִׁמְךָ הַגָּדוֹל:

Bimei matityahu ben yochanan kohen gadol chashmonai uvanav keshe'amda malchut yavan harsha'a al amecha yisra'el lehashkicham toratecha ulha'aviram mechukei retzonecha. Ve'ata berachamecha harabim amadta lahem be'et tzaratam ravta et rivam danta et dinam nakamta et nikmatam masarta giborim beyad chalashim verabim beyad me'atim utme'im beyad tehorim ursha'im beyad tzadikim vezedim beyad oskei toratecha ulcha asita shem gadol vekadosh be'olamecha ul'amecha yisra'el asita teshu'a gedola ufurkan kehayom haze ve'achar kach ba'u vanecha lidvir beitecha ufinu et heichalecha vetiharu et mikdashecha vehidliku nerot bechatzrot kodshecha vekav'u shemonat yemei chanuka elu lehodot ulhalel leshimcha hagadol.

In the days of Matisyahu, son of Yochanan the High Priest, the Hasmonean and his sons, when the evil Greek kingdom rose up against Your people Israel to make them forget Your Torah and to turn them away from the statutes of Your will— You, in Your abundant mercy, stood by them in their time of distress, You defended their cause, You judged their grievances, You avenged them. The four blessings

Birkat Hamazon is built of four blessings, and the siddur itself notes who composed each one. The first, which ends “hazan et hakol”, thanks God who feeds all – composed by Moshe when the manna came down. The second, “al ha'aretz ve'al hamazon”, gives thanks for the Land, the covenant and the Torah – composed by Yehoshua when Israel entered the Land. The third, “boneh Yerushalayim”, asks for Jerusalem – composed by David and Shlomo. The fourth, “HaTov VeHaMetiv”, was composed in Yavneh. After them come the HaRachaman requests. Questions and answers

What is Birkat Hamazon?

Birkat Hamazon, the Grace After Meals, is the blessing said after a meal with bread. It is made of four blessings followed by the HaRachaman requests, and its source is the verse וְאָכַלְתָּ וְשָׂבָעְתָּ וּבֵרַכְתָּ אֶת־ה' אֱלֹקֶיךָ עַל־הָאָרֶץ הַטֹּבָה אֲשֶׁר נָתַן־לָךְ: (Deuteronomy 8:10). In English it is also called bentching, and the booklet it is printed in is called a bentcher.

When do you say Birkat Hamazon?

After eating bread. The Kitzur Shulchan Aruch writes: אֲפִלּוּ לֹא אָכַל רַק כַּזַּיִת פַּת, צָרִיךְ לְבָרֵךְ בִּרְכַּת הַמָּזוֹן (Kitzur Shulchan Aruch 44:5) – in plain words: even someone who ate only an olive-size piece of bread says Birkat Hamazon. After grain foods, wine or fruit of the seven species the after-blessing is Me'ein Shalosh, and after other foods Borei Nefashot. Both are on the page.

How long after eating can you still say it?

עד אימתי יכול לברך עד שיתעכל המזון שבמעיו וכמה שיעורו כל זמן שאינו רעב מחמת אותה אכילה (Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chaim 184:5) – in plain words: until the food is digested, which means as long as one is not hungry again from that meal. And the Kitzur Shulchan Aruch: יֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים, דְּשִׁעוּר עִכּוּל הוּא שָׁעָה וְחֹמֶשׁ (44:8) – some say the time of digestion is an hour and a fifth. With a practical question, ask a rabbi.

How many people are needed for a zimun?

שְׁלשָׁה שֶׁאָכְלוּ בְּיַחַד צְרִיכִין לְבָרֵךְ בְּזִמּוּן (Kitzur Shulchan Aruch 45:1) – in plain words: three who ate together bless with a zimun. אִם הֵם עֲשָׂרָה, נִתְחַיְבוּ לְבָרֵךְ בְּשֵׁם (45:12) – if they are ten, the Name is added, the word “E-loheinu”. The text of the zimun is at the top of the page.

I forgot Retzeh on Shabbat. What now?

The Kitzur Shulchan Aruch: אִם נִזְכַּר קֹדֶם שֶׁהִזְכִּיר אֶת הַשֵּׁם מִן הַבְּרָכָה שֶׁל בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה' בּוֹנֶה בְּרַחֲמָיו יְרוּשָׁלָיִם, אוֹמֵר שָׁם רְצֵה אוֹ יַעֲלֶה וְיָבֹא (44:12) – in plain words: someone who remembered before saying the Name in the blessing “Boneh Yerushalayim” says Retzeh or Ya'aleh VeYavo there. For someone who remembered only later, the page has the part “If you forgot” with the text that is said. With any practical doubt, ask a rabbi.

I forgot Al HaNisim on Chanukah or Purim?

The Kitzur Shulchan Aruch: אַךְ בְּתוֹךְ הָרַחֲמָן יֹאמַר, הָרַחֲמָן הוּא יַעֲשֶׂה לָנוּ נִסִּים וְנִפְלָאוֹת, כְּמוֹ שֶׁעָשָׂה לַאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בַּיָּמִים הַהֵם בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה (44:16) – in plain words: it is said among the HaRachaman requests, as a request that the Merciful One do miracles and wonders for us as He did for our ancestors in those days at this time.
