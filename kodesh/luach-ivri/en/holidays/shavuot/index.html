About Shavuot

Shavuot 2027 falls on June 11, 2027 – June 12, 2027 and begins on the Thursday evening, June 10, 2027. Last time it was on May 22, 2026 – May 23, 2026. Below is general background about the holiday: what it celebrates, its main customs and why its date changes from year to year. It is a short introduction, not a guide to practice; for questions about how to keep the holiday, ask a rabbi.

What Shavuot celebrates

Shavuot, the Festival of Weeks, falls on the 6th of Sivan, at the end of the seven weeks of the Omer count that begins on Passover. It marks the giving of the Torah at Mount Sinai. It is also the festival of the wheat harvest and of the first fruits, which were brought to the Temple in Jerusalem.

One day in Israel, two days outside

In Israel Shavuot is one day of Yom Tov. Outside Israel it lasts two days, the 6th and 7th of Sivan. Both are Yom Tov, days on which work is not done. Like every holiday, it begins on the evening before the first date.

Learning through the night

Many stay awake on the first night of Shavuot learning Torah until dawn, a custom called Tikkun Leil Shavuot, and then pray the morning prayer at sunrise. In the synagogue the Ten Commandments are read from the Torah, and the Book of Ruth is read too.

Dairy foods and greenery

It is customary to eat dairy foods on Shavuot, such as cheesecake and blintzes, and many decorate the home and the synagogue with flowers and greenery. In Israel, kibbutzim and schools hold first-fruits festivals.

Counting to Shavuot

Shavuot is the only holiday that the Torah does not tie to a date in the month but to a count: fifty days from the second day of Passover. You can follow the count each evening on the page Omer count tonight.

Why Shavuot begins in the evening

On the Hebrew calendar each day begins at sunset, not at midnight. So Shavuot begins on the evening before the first date in the tables, and ends at nightfall on its last day. That is why the box at the top of this page shows both the date and the evening it begins. Candle lighting is usually about 18 minutes before sunset, and each city has its own times.

Questions and answers about Shavuot

When is Shavuot 2027?

Shavuot 2027: June 11, 2027 – June 12, 2027, two days outside Israel and one day in Israel. It begins on the Thursday evening, June 10, 2027, at sunset. These are the dates outside Israel; the Israel dates are in the table on this page.

When is Shavuot 2028?

Shavuot 2028: May 31, 2028 – June 1, 2028, beginning on the Tuesday evening, May 30, 2028.

When was Shavuot 2026?

Shavuot 2026 was on May 22, 2026 – May 23, 2026, beginning on the Thursday evening, May 21, 2026.

What time does Shavuot start?

Like every Jewish holiday, Shavuot begins at sunset on the evening before its first date, and ends at nightfall. The exact minute depends on your city. For local times see candle lighting times or ask your synagogue.

Why does Shavuot fall on a different date every year?

Shavuot always has the same date on the Hebrew calendar, which follows the moon and adds a leap month in some years. On the Gregorian calendar the date moves by up to a few weeks. You can convert any date with the Hebrew date converter.

How many days is Shavuot?

Two days outside Israel and one day in Israel.

Why is dairy eaten on Shavuot?

It is a widespread custom with several reasons given, among them that when the Torah was given, with its laws of kosher meat, the people ate dairy. Cheesecake and blintzes are favorites.
