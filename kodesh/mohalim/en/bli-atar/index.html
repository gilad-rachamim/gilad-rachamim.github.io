The brit date

In brief: the eighth day, a birth before or after sunset, the jaundice test, Shabbat, festivals and a cesarean birth. With fields to fill in.

Word (Hebrew)PDF to print (Hebrew)
WhatsApp template
*📅 The brit date* ━━━━━━━━━━ According to Jewish law, the brit milah is held on the eighth day after a son is born, and this is also the most recommended time for his health. *🌅 The brit day* The brit milah is held on the eighth day after the birth: one week after the day of birth, on the same day of the week. If the baby was born before sunset, the day of birth counts as day one. If he was born after sunset, the count starts from the next day. *🩺 Jaundice test* The baby should have a jaundice test, and you should tell the mohel the result and the day and time of the test, because it makes a difference. *🕯️ Shabbat, festivals and a cesarean birth* A brit on time, on the eighth day, is held even on Shabbat and on a festival. A brit that is not on time, for example after a cesarean birth or after a delay because of jaundice, does not override Shabbat, so it moves to a weekday. ━━━━━━━━━━ *✍️ Our details* 📅 Date of birth: 🕰️ Time of birth: 🌅 Before sunset / after sunset: 🏥 Caesarean birth: yes / no 🩺 Jaundice test (result, day and time): ━━━━━━━━━━ In some cases the brit is not held on time even when the baby is healthy, so you must speak with the mohel in advance. _From the free tools of Gilad Rachamim, a certified mohel and member of the mohels' forum_

The brit day

What to bring, and how to get the baby ready.

PDF to print (Hebrew)

*🎉 The brit day: what to bring* ━━━━━━━━━━ *What is needed for the brit itself* ⬜ Brit pillow ⬜ Kiddush cup and dry red wine ⬜ 2 tallitot (one for the father and one for the sandak) ⬜ Fragrant spices, preferably myrtle (the custom of Sephardim and Mizrahi communities) ⬜ A siddur for the order of blessings, unless the mohel brings one for you *👕 Clothes for the baby* A two-piece outfit, so the bottoms can be taken off while the baby keeps a vest or top on. That way he is not fully undressed and does not get cold. *🍼 Feeding the baby* Last feed about an hour before the brit. In the last hour you no longer feed him, and until then let him eat as much as he likes. *🎒 Diaper bag* Cloth diapers, disposable diapers, wipes, a change of clothes just in case, formula if he is not breastfed, a pacifier, diaper cream. ━━━━━━━━━━ And most important: come in a good mood, and leave all the tiredness behind. _From the free tools of Gilad Rachamim, a certified mohel and member of the mohels' forum_

After the brit

The instructions day by day, and when to call at once.

PDF to print (Hebrew)
WhatsApp template
*🩹 After the brit: the instructions* ━━━━━━━━━━ *📍 On the brit day* ▫️ After every diaper change, drip natural oil on the dressing. ▫️ Check that the baby is passing urine (look in the diaper). ▫️ You can wash the baby about eight hours after the brit. ▫️ A little blood in the diaper is no reason to worry. Only if there is a lot of blood, contact the mohel for instructions. ▫️ For pain relief you can give Novimol syrup every five or six hours. *📍 The day after the brit* ▫️ It is very good to bathe the baby (including the brit area) a few times: the water softens the dressing. ▫️ After the bath, drip natural oil on the dressing: almond oil or mineral oil (paraffin oil) (if you have none, a good olive oil). ▫️ The mohel comes the day after the brit to remove the dressing. Once it is off, a whitish-yellow discharge appears around the cut. This is natural and part of the healing. *📍 After the mohel removes the dressing* ▫️ Treat the baby as usual. ▫️ Apply Bepanthen Plus or mupirocin around the brit area twice a day for three days. Do not overdo it: too much ointment is not healthy. If it seems more is needed, only in coordination with the mohel. ▫️ Bathe the baby twice a day for five days. *🚨 When to call at once* ▫️ Bleeding that does not stop, or a blood stain that keeps growing. ▫️ The baby has not passed urine for many hours after the brit. ▫️ Fever, swelling or redness that spreads. ▫️ Anything that worries you. No question is unnecessary. ━━━━━━━━━━ "When the mother is calm, the baby is calm too" _These instructions do not replace the pediatrician: with any health concern, fever or bleeding that does not stop, go to a doctor or the emergency room._ _From the free tools of Gilad Rachamim, a certified mohel and member of the mohels' forum_
