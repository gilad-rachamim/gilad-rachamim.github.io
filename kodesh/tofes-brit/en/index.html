A brit milah form for parents, straight to the mohel's WhatsApp

The parents fill in the details of the birth on their phone: date, time, hospital, weight, jaundice and cesarean. Then the details of the brit: place, time, nusach and sandak. The message goes to the mohel's WhatsApp, neatly arranged.

A personal link for the mohel

The mohel writes his number once and gets a link. The parents open it, fill it in, and the message comes straight to him. For those who prefer another way, there is a WhatsApp template and a Word file.

The details are not saved by us anywhere, only on the parents' phone until they send. More for mohels: the brit log and the mohel's business card.

What the parents fill in

Name and phone, date and time of birth, the hospital from a list, and the medical details the mohel needs, such as jaundice and cesarean. Then the details of the brit: place, time, nusach and sandak. At the end they tap, and the message opens in the mohel's WhatsApp, neat and readable.

What the mohel gets

Write your number once and get a personal link. Send it to the parents after the first call. That way all the details arrive in one place, with no searching through messages and no asking again.

Privacy

The form does not save the details on the site. The message goes straight from the parents' phone to the mohel's WhatsApp.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The tool is built as an act of kindness, with no ads and no collection of details. Use it as much as you like, and send it to the gabbai, the rabbi or friends.

Fewer phone calls before the brit

Instead of asking the parents by phone about the baby's name, the father's name, the time of birth, the place of the brit and the honors, the mohel sends one link, the parents fill it in at their ease, and the details reach him neatly on WhatsApp. That way there are also no mistakes in the spelling of the names for "Vayikare Shemo BeYisrael". And for the invitation itself: an invitation to the brit.

Questions and answers

What does the mohel need to know before the brit?

Date and time of birth, hospital, weight, jaundice, cesarean, place and time of the brit, nusach and sandak. All of these are in the form.

How does the form reach the mohel?

By tapping "Send to the mohel on WhatsApp". All that is left is to tap Send there.

Are the details saved on the site?

No. They stay on the phone until sending.

How does a mohel make his own link?

He writes his number at the bottom of the page and gets a link to send to the parents.

How does the mohel receive the details?

The parents fill in the form on their phone, and the details are sent as a neat WhatsApp message to the mohel's number.

Is this page free?

Yes. Everything here is free, with no sign-up and no ads.
