Friday night Kiddush

Said at the table on Friday night, over a full cup of wine, before the meal. The one making Kiddush says it aloud, and everyone listening answers Amen after each blessing.

Quietly

וַיְהִי עֶֽרֶב וַיְהִי בֹֽקֶר

Say: Vay'hi erev vay'hi voker

Meaning: And evening came, then morning came:

Aloud – Vayechulu (Genesis 2:1-3)

יוֹם הַשִּׁשִּׁי: וַיְכֻלּוּ הַשָּׁמַֽיִם וְהָאָֽרֶץ וְכָל־צְבָאָם: וַיְכַל אֱלֹהִים בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי מְלַאכְתּוֹ אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה. וַיִּשְׁבֹּת בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי מִכָּל־מְלַאכְתּוֹ אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה: וַיְבָֽרֶךְ אֱלֹהִים אֶת־יוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי וַיְקַדֵּשׁ אֹתוֹ. כִּי בוֹ שָׁבַת מִכָּל מְלַאכְתּוֹ אֲשֶׁר־בָּרָא אֱלֹהִים לַעֲשׂוֹת:

Say: Yom hashishi. Vay'chulu hashamayim veha'aretz v'chol tz'va'am. Vay'chal E-lohim bayom hash'vi'i m'lachto asher asa. Vayishbot bayom hash'vi'i mikol m'lachto asher asa. Vay'varech E-lohim et yom hash'vi'i vay'kadesh oto. Ki vo shavat mikol m'lachto asher bara E-lohim la'asot.

Meaning: the sixth day. The heavens and the earth were completed and [so were] all their hosts. And God completed, by the seventh day, His work which He had done; and He abstained on the seventh day from all His work which He had done. And God blessed the seventh day and sanctified it, for on it He abstained from all His work which God had created to do.

Savri

סַבְרִי מָרָנָן וְרַבּוֹתַי:

Say: Savri maranan v'rabotai.

Meaning: Attention! our masters and our teachers:

The blessing over wine

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה' אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ מֶֽלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם בּוֹרֵא פְּרִי הַגָּֽפֶן:

Say: Baruch ata A-donai eloheinu melech ha'olam borei p'ri hagafen.

Meaning: Blessed are You, Adonoy our God, King of the Universe, Creator of the fruit of the vine.

Everyone answers: Amen

The Kiddush blessing

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה' אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ מֶֽלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָֽׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֹתָיו וְרָֽצָה בָֽנוּ, וְשַׁבַּת קָדְשׁוֹ בְּאַהֲבָה וּבְרָצוֹן הִנְחִילָֽנוּ, זִכָּרוֹן לְמַעֲשֵׂה בְרֵאשִׁית. (כִּי הוּא יוֹם) תְּחִלָּה לְמִקְרָאֵי קֹֽדֶשׁ, זֵֽכֶר לִיצִיאַת מִצְרָֽיִם. (כִּי בָֽנוּ בָחַֽרְתָּ וְאוֹתָֽנוּ קִדַּֽשְׁתָּ מִכָּל הָעַמִּים) וְשַׁבַּת קָדְשְׁךָ בְּאַהֲבָה וּבְרָצוֹן הִנְחַלְתָּֽנוּ. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה', מְקַדֵּשׁ הַשַּׁבָּת:

Say: Baruch ata A-donai eloheinu melech ha'olam, asher kid'shanu b'mitzvotav v'ratza vanu, v'shabbat kodsho b'ahava uvratzon hinchilanu, zikaron l'ma'aseh v'reishit. (Ki hu yom) t'chila l'mikra'ei kodesh, zecher litzi'at mitzrayim. (Ki vanu vacharta v'otanu kidashta mikol ha'amim) v'shabbat kodshecha b'ahava uvratzon hinchaltanu. Baruch ata A-donai, m'kadesh hashabbat.

Meaning: Blessed are You, Adonoy our God, King of the Universe, Who sanctified us with His commandments and was pleased with us. And His holy Sabbath, with love and pleasure, He gave us for a heritage, a commemoration of the work of creation. (For this day is) the first of the days of holy convocations commemorating the exodus from Egypt. (For us have You chosen and us have You sanctified from among all the peoples.) And Your holy Sabbath with love and pleasure You gave us for a heritage. Blessed are You, Adonoy, Sanctifier of the Sabbath.

The words in brackets are printed in brackets in the Metsudah siddur.

Everyone answers: Amen

Shabbat day Kiddush (Kiddusha Rabba)

Said on Shabbat morning after the prayers, before the meal, over a cup of wine.

V’shamru (Exodus 31:16-17)

וְשָׁמְרוּ בְנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל אֶת־הַשַּׁבָּת לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת־הַשַּׁבָּת לְדֹרֹתָם בְּרִית עוֹלָם׃ בֵּינִי וּבֵין בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל אוֹת הִוא לְעֹלָם כִּי־שֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים עָשָׂה ה' אֶת־הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֶת־הָאָרֶץ וּבַיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי שָׁבַת וַיִּנָּפַשׁ׃

Say: V'shamru v'nei yisra'el et hashabbat la'asot et hashabbat l'dorotam b'rit olam. Beini uvein b'nei yisra'el ot hi le'olam ki sheshet yamim asa A-donai et hashamayim ve'et ha'aretz uvayom hash'vi'i shavat vayinafash.

Meaning: Wherefore the children of Israel shall keep the sabbath, to observe the sabbath throughout their generations, for a perpetual covenant. It is a sign between Me and the children of Israel for ever; for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day He ceased from work and rested.

Zachor (Exodus 20:8-11)

זָכוֹר אֶת־יוֹם הַשַּׁבָּת לְקַדְּשׁוֹ שֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים תַּעֲבֹד וְעָשִׂיתָ כָּל־מְלַאכְתֶּךָ וְיוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי שַׁבָּת לַה' אֱלֹהֶיךָ לֹא־תַעֲשֶׂה כָל־מְלָאכָה אַתָּה וּבִנְךָ־וּבִתֶּךָ עַבְדְּךָ וַאֲמָתְךָ וּבְהֶמְתֶּךָ וְגֵרְךָ אֲשֶׁר בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָ כִּי שֵׁשֶׁת־יָמִים עָשָׂה ה' אֶת־הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֶת־הָאָרֶץ אֶת־הַיָּם וְאֶת־כָּל־אֲשֶׁר־בָּם וַיָּנַח בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי עַל־כֵּן בֵּרַךְ ה' אֶת־יוֹם הַשַּׁבָּת וַיְקַדְּשֵׁהוּ׃

Say: Zachor et yom hashabbat l'kad'sho sheshet yamim ta'avod ve'asita kol melachtecha veyom hash'vi'i shabbat la'A-donai E-lohecha lo ta'ase chol melacha ata uvincha uvitecha avdecha va'amatcha uvhemtecha vegercha asher bish'arecha ki sheshet yamim asa A-donai et hashamayim ve'et ha'aretz et hayam ve'et kol asher bam vayanach bayom hash'vi'i al ken berach A-donai et yom hashabbat vay'kad'shehu.

Meaning: Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work; but the seventh day is a sabbath unto the LORD thy God, in it thou shalt not do any manner of work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy man-servant, nor thy maid-servant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates; for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested on the seventh day; wherefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.

The blessing over wine

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה' אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ מֶֽלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם בּוֹרֵא פְּרִי הַגָּֽפֶן:

Say: Baruch ata A-donai eloheinu melech ha'olam borei p'ri hagafen.

Meaning: Blessed are You, Adonoy our God, King of the Universe, Creator of the fruit of the vine.

Everyone answers: Amen

How to make Kiddush on Friday night

1. Set the table

Before Kiddush the table is set for the Shabbat meal, with two whole challah loaves covered by a cloth, and the Shabbat candles already lit. Have a cup ready that holds enough wine or grape juice for everyone to taste.

2. Fill the cup and hold it

The one making Kiddush fills the cup and holds it in front of everyone. Those at the table listen quietly, because by listening and answering Amen they take part in the Kiddush.

3. Say the text aloud

Start with “Yom hashishi, Vay'chulu”, the verses from Genesis about God completing the world and resting on the seventh day. Then “Savri maranan” and the blessing over wine, and then the long Kiddush blessing, which ends “Baruch ata A-donai, m'kadesh hashabbat”. On this page each part is in its own box, so you can follow it slowly.

4. Drink, and wash for the meal

After the blessing the one who made Kiddush drinks from the cup and passes it around, or pours a little for everyone. Then everyone washes their hands for bread and the meal begins with the blessing over the challah.

Questions of Jewish law

How much wine to drink, whether to stand or sit, who can make Kiddush for whom, and what to do without wine are questions of Jewish law, with different customs between communities. Ask an Orthodox rabbi about your own home.

How to make Kiddush on Shabbat day

On Shabbat morning, after the prayers, Kiddush is made again before the meal. It is shorter: the verses “V'shamru” (Exodus 31) and “Zachor” (Exodus 20), and the blessing over wine. Many synagogues hold a “Kiddush” after the prayers on Shabbat morning, a light meal that begins with this Kiddush, and the name stuck to the whole event.

What the Kiddush means

Remembering creation

The Ten Commandments say “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy”. The Kiddush is how we remember it: with words, over a cup of wine, at the start of the day. The verses Vay'chulu tell that God finished the heavens and the earth and rested on the seventh day.

Remembering the exodus

The Kiddush blessing calls Shabbat “a remembrance of the work of creation” and also “a remembrance of the exodus from Egypt”. Shabbat is a gift given “with love and pleasure”, a sign between God and the people of Israel.

Why wine

Wine is used for blessings and joyful moments in Jewish life, such as weddings and a brit. Starting Shabbat over a cup of wine marks it as a special, joyful day.

Reading the transliteration

The transliteration spells each word in English letters, the way it is said in most synagogues. “ch” sounds like the end of “Bach”, “tz” like the end of “cats”, and an apostrophe marks a very short vowel, as in m'kadesh. The Name of God is written “A-donai” and “E-lohim” out of respect. To learn the Hebrew letters, see the Hebrew alphabet page.

Questions and answers

What are the words of the Kiddush?

On Friday night the Kiddush has three parts: the verses "Vay'chulu" from Genesis about the seventh day, the blessing over wine "Baruch ata A-donai, Eloheinu melech ha'olam, borei p'ri hagafen", and the Kiddush blessing that ends "Baruch ata A-donai, m'kadesh hashabbat" (Blessed are You, who sanctifies the Shabbat). On Shabbat day the Kiddush is the verses "V'shamru" and "Zachor" and the blessing over wine. All of it is on this page in Hebrew, transliteration and English.

What does the Kiddush mean?

Kiddush means "sanctification". Over a cup of wine we declare that Shabbat is holy: God finished creating the world and rested on the seventh day, He gave us the Shabbat with love, and it is a remembrance of the exodus from Egypt. The English line under each Hebrew line on this page gives the meaning word for word.

How do you say Kiddush if you cannot read Hebrew?

Read the line marked "Say" under each Hebrew line: it spells the Hebrew words in English letters. "ch" sounds like the end of the Scottish word loch, and an apostrophe marks a very short vowel. Press "Big print" to read from your phone at the table, or print the page.

What is the procedure for reciting the Friday night Kiddush?

The table is set, the challah is covered, and a cup of wine is filled. The one making Kiddush holds the cup, says the text aloud, and everyone listening answers Amen after each blessing. Then he drinks from the wine and the others taste from it, and everyone washes for the meal. Customs differ, such as standing or sitting; follow your family's custom or ask your rabbi.

Can a woman make Kiddush?

This is a question of Jewish law. Ask an Orthodox rabbi about your own situation; he will explain who can make Kiddush for whom.

What can you use if you have no wine?

Wine or grape juice is the usual cup. What to do without wine, and how much to drink, are questions of Jewish law; ask your rabbi.

What is the difference between the Friday night and the Shabbat day Kiddush?

The Friday night Kiddush includes a full blessing that sanctifies the day. The Shabbat day Kiddush, called Kiddusha Rabba ("the great Kiddush"), is shorter: Torah verses about Shabbat and the blessing over wine. Both are on this page.

What does Savri maranan mean?

"Savri maranan v'rabotai" means "Attention, our masters and our teachers". It is said before the blessing over wine, to call those at the table to listen.

Is there Kiddush on holidays?

Yes, on the festivals there is a Kiddush with its own text. This page has the Shabbat Kiddush only; use the siddur of your synagogue for the festivals.
